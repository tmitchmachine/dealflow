# Dealflow

Dealflow helps people find businesses for sale and helps owners sell them.

## What it does

- **Find businesses for sale.** Browse listings of companies that are on the market, with enough context to decide whether a deal is worth a closer look.
- **Help sell a business.** Give owners a place to present their company to buyers who are actively looking.

## Status

This is the starting point for the project. More will come as the product takes shape.
